Criminal Charges

Being charged with a crime can be overwhelming. Our attorneys provide strong, strategic defense to protect your rights and guide you through every stage of the legal process.

We handle cases involving:

  • Assault
  • Theft & property crimes
  • Drug possession & distribution
  • DWI / DUI
  • Domestic violence
  • Weapons charges
  • Juvenile offenses

We examine evidence, challenge improper procedures, negotiate with prosecutors, and fight for dismissals or reduced charges whenever possible.

False Arrest

If you were arrested without probable cause, you may be entitled to compensation. False arrest cases often involve violations of constitutional rights, improper police conduct, or mistaken identity.

We help clients pursue claims for:

  • Wrongful detention
  • Unlawful search and seizure
  • Improper police procedures
  • Emotional and financial damages

Our team investigates the circumstances of your arrest and works to hold responsible parties accountable.

Police Misconduct

Police misconduct can include excessive force, harassment, discrimination, or abuse of authority. These cases require careful documentation and strong legal advocacy.

Examples include:

  • Excessive force
  • Racial profiling
  • Unlawful stops
  • Improper searches
  • Coercive interrogation

We help clients file complaints, pursue civil claims, and seek justice for violations of their rights.
